How to play

EvoKingdoms is a small living world. Watch organisms evolve from a handful of cells, then guide humans, kingdoms and wars into being.

Eras

  1. Primordial — only single-cell organisms exist. Producers make food, mouths eat it, movers crawl, eyes see, killers kill, armor protects. Every reproduction mutates a copy.
  2. Sentient — once an organism reaches enough cells (or you press Advance Era), humans awaken. They forage, reproduce, found kingdoms, build villages → towns → castles, and wage war.

Controls

  • Pick a Tool then click / drag on the world.
  • Press Inspect + click to read a tile's full info.
  • Trigger Disasters for chaos. The world remembers.
  • Use the Speed slider to fast-forward eras.

Tips

  • Paint a small lake with the Water brush — coastline life tends to be more interesting.
  • Drop Food on starving colonies to nurture them.
  • Surround a kingdom with mountains to slow its expansion.
